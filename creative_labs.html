Full Stack Creative Labs is an in-house development division whose objective is to research problems existing within public and private sectors of society and determine how technology can be used to bring about a solution to those problems. We are always looking for ways to use technology to make life easier, more efficient and more enjoyable for people in all industries and walks of life. Below is a list of projects currently in development at Full Stack Creative Labs.

AI Neuro Assistant

Loadless is an AI-powered neuro-assistant designed to help individuals diagnosed with Autism Spectrum Disorder, Level 1, (ASD-1), manage the daily challenges they face in their personal and professional lives. The first version of Loadless is out of development and currently being prepared for beta testing.

SUNSEIZER

Tech Pack System

This application is a collaboration between Seizer Change, chief designer for the PMPNU Clothing brand and Full Stack Creative Labs. Sunseizer is a PLM (Product Lifecycle Management) software solution that is sure to revolutionize manufacturing in the fashion industry.

Omaha's Black Business Directory

OBBD is a vehicle through which to bring more awareness and patronage to the Black-owned businesses in Omaha, NE. Other organizations have attempted to develop directories for black-owned businesses, but none will match what is being created at Full Stack. This will be the official Black Business Directory for the Omaha area.

StockSync is an app that automates the ordering process for a retail store environment. Based on distributor contact information, products carried by a particular distributor, contracted ordering requirements, the location where orders are placed, and weekly inventory, StockSync will tell you what and how much will need to be ordered and handle the rest.
